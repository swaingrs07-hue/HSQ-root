What’s Working

* H Square has a memorable brand name.
* You already have multiple properties.
* You’re building tech (Hsquare Connect app), which most hostel operators don’t have.
* You have a hospitality angle, not just a real-estate angle.

What’s Not Working

1. Your positioning is unclear.
    * Are you a hostel?
    * A PG?
    * Student housing?
    * Co-living?
    * Serviced accommodation?
    If customers can’t explain H Square in one sentence, marketing becomes expensive.
2. Student Housing owns the keyword “student housing”.
    * They benefit from brand-name and keyword overlap.
    * If someone searches “student housing Mumbai”, they have an advantage.
3. Your website likely focuses too much on properties and too little on outcomes.
    * Students want lifestyle.
    * Parents want safety.
    * Neither cares much about room dimensions.
4. Reviews are probably under-leveraged.
    * Hundreds of genuine Google reviews can outperform large ad budgets.

What I Would Do Next 90 Days

1. Own NMIMS Search Traffic

Create the best pages for:

* Student Hostel Near NMIMS
* Accommodation Near NMIMS
* Boys Hostel Near NMIMS
* Girls Hostel Near NMIMS

These are high-intent searches.

2. Turn Hsquare Connect into a Selling Point

Most hostel websites say:

Wi-Fi, Laundry, Meals

You should say:

Smart student living with app-based meal tracking, maintenance requests, attendance, transport, and community updates.

That’s differentiation.

3. Build Social Proof

Film:

* Resident room tours
* Parent testimonials
* Move-in day videos
* Festival celebrations
* Student success stories

Post daily.

4. Fix the Homepage Message

If your homepage doesn’t explain the value in 5 seconds, it loses visitors.

Example:

Premium Student Living in Mumbai
Fully managed accommodation near top colleges with meals, housekeeping, security, and a resident app.

5. Create One Flagship Property

Have one property so good that every marketing campaign points to it.
People remember brands through flagship experiences.

Biggest Threat

Your biggest competitor isn’t Student Housing.

It’s:

* Local PGs charging less
* Students finding roommates and renting apartments
* Empty beds caused by poor online visibility

Biggest Opportunity

You already have:

* Properties
* Brand
* App
* Operations

Most hostel operators have only properties.

If executed well, H Square can become a student-living platform, not just a hostel company.

The question I’d ask is:
How many occupied beds does H Square currently have, and what percentage occupancy are you running at? That determines whether the priority is SEO, branding, sales, pricing, or operations.
