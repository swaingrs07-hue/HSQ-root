🧩 DISCOUNT TYPES

1. Base Discounts
- Flat % discount (e.g., 10% off)
- Fixed discount (₹500 off)

2. Conditional Discounts
- Stay 3 nights → get 1 free
- Book before X days → early bird discount
- Last-minute deals (within 24–48 hrs)

3. Occupancy-Based Discounts (AUTO)
- If occupancy < 40% → auto apply discount
- If high occupancy → remove discounts

4. Time-Based Discounts
- Weekday offers
- Weekend surge pricing (inverse discount)

5. Seasonal Campaigns
- Festival offers (Diwali, New Year)
- Monsoon sale / Summer deals
