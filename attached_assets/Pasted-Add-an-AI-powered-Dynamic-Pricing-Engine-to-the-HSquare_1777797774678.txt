Add an AI-powered Dynamic Pricing Engine to the HSquare Harmony Living system.

--------------------------------------

🎯 OBJECTIVE

Automatically suggest optimal room/bed pricing based on:
- Demand
- Occupancy rate
- Seasonality
- Local events
- Booking trends

--------------------------------------

📊 DATA INPUTS

The AI model should use:

1. Internal Data:
- Current occupancy rate (%)
- Total bookings (last 7 / 30 days)
- Room type demand (Dorm vs Private)
- Remaining inventory

2. Time-Based Factors:
- Day of week (weekend vs weekday)
- Season/month trends
- Upcoming holidays

3. External Factors (optional API-ready):
- Local events (festivals, concerts)
- Competitor pricing (if available)

--------------------------------------

🧠 PRICING LOGIC (INITIAL RULE-BASED AI)

If Occupancy > 80%:
→ Increase price by 15–25%

If Occupancy < 40%:
→ Decrease price by 10–20%

Weekend:
→ Increase by 10–15%

High Demand Room Type:
→ Increase price dynamically

Low Demand Inventory:
→ Offer discount suggestions

--------------------------------------

📈 MACHINE LEARNING (ADVANCED)

- Track historical pricing vs booking success
- Learn which prices convert best
- Predict future demand trends
- Suggest:
  - Optimal price
  - Discount campaigns
  - Surge pricing
